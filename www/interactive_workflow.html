Important interpretation rules

Use genotype replication and an appropriate design for variance components. The core visualization PCA and range retention are descriptive, not proof of allele conservation. Dendrograms based on traits are not phylogenetic trees. The SampleCore and Core Hunter integrations require separate installations. Several advanced methods need external numerical benchmarking.
